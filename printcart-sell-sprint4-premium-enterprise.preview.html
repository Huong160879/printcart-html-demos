Plan questions

What does Store Premium include?

Store Premium provides the configured Merchant Store, publishing and supported connection benefits. AI processing is funded separately through the shared user token wallet.

How do AI tokens work?

Tokens belong to the authenticated Printcart user and can be used by supported AI tools on Printcart.com and connected plugins. Each task shows its cost before confirmation.

How can I add tokens?

Use an existing non-recurring token top-up or the existing monthly recurring token subscription. Both fund the same wallet; they do not create separate balances for individual tools.

Does buying tokens upgrade my store?

No. Token purchases fund AI usage only. Upgrade Store Premium separately when a Merchant Store capability requires it.

Does Store Premium include unlimited AI?

No. Any included credit must come from the approved central billing policy. This prototype does not invent a Remove BG allowance for Store Premium.

Which store connections are supported?

Only destinations confirmed in configuration are shown. This prototype uses Demo store and does not connect a real platform.

How does monthly or annual Store Premium billing work?

Store Premium may be billed monthly or yearly according to the existing Merchant Store catalog. Token subscription billing is managed separately in the shared wallet.

HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently asked questions

Background removal, tokens and result reuse explained.

Can I try background removal for free?

A new account includes 3 background removals, up to 10 saved projects and up to 5 published listings. The removal trial is available for 30 days after it is granted.

Do I need an account?

You can upload and preview without an account. Sign in or create an account to download the PNG and receive 3 free removals.

Will my download have a transparent background?

After successful processing, Download PNG saves the actual result with transparent areas. Preview backgrounds are not added to the exported file.

When does a removal require tokens?

After the free allowance is used, the next removal requires a confirmed cost from the shared Printcart Token Wallet. No token is used before confirmation.

What happens when my free allowance is used?

Your current PNG remains available. A new image waits while you review its cost, wallet balance and any funding options.

Can I continue in Online Design?

Use in Online Design preserves the PNG and its result identity so you can continue without uploading it again.

How are my images stored and removed?

Originals and preview-only results are not added to My Images. A result enters My Images only after Download PNG.

Does using the result again consume more removal tokens?

No. Download, My Images, Online Design, Product, Save, Publish and Sync reuse the committed result without running background removal again.
